Passport Photo Coach

Checks a New Zealand passport photo against the Department of Internal Affairs rules, then crops it to the exact file DIA asks for. Private test version. Not a government service.

Live guide: tap Start camera and allow camera access. Photos never leave this phone.

Loading the face checker…
Someone else holds the phone at the person's eye level. First the person stands against the wall so the app can measure the half-metre gap.
It takes the photo by itself when every check is green.

Take the photo with the phone's normal camera (best quality), or pick one you already have.

Checks

    Your passport file

    Before you apply, run it through DIA's own online photo checker on passports.govt.nz. A passport officer still makes the final call.

    The rules this checks
    • Taken by someone else from about 1.5 m, camera at eye level. No selfies.
    • Stand about 0.5 m in front of a plain, light background (pale grey or blue works better than white). No shadows behind the head.
    • Face straight on, not tilted or turned. Eyes open, mouth closed, neutral expression.
    • Even light on the face, ideally coming from behind and above the photographer. Don't stand right under a light.
    • Hair out of the eyes. Shoulders, upper chest and a gap around the head, hair and ears.
    • Prescription glasses only if not tinted or thick-framed, with no glare. No sunglasses. Hats only for religious or medical reasons.
    • File: colour JPG, 3:4, 900–4500 px wide by 1200–6000 px high, 250 KB to 5 MB. No edits, filters or AI changes to how you look: this app only crops and resizes.

    Source: passports.govt.nz passport photo page and DIA's iPhone and Samsung guides, read 17 November 2026.
