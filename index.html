A plain, light wallPale grey or blue works better than white. Nothing behind your head.

All checks
    The rules this checks
    • Taken by someone else from about 1.5 m, camera at eye level. No selfies.
    • About 0.5 m in front of a plain, light background. No shadows behind the head.
    • Face straight on, not tilted or turned. Eyes open, mouth closed, neutral expression.
    • Even light on the face. Hair out of the eyes. Shoulders, upper chest and a gap around the head.
    • Prescription glasses only if not tinted or thick-framed, no glare. Hats only for religious or medical reasons.
    • Colour JPG, 3:4, 900–4500 px wide by 1200–6000 px high, 250 KB–5 MB. No edits or filters: this app only crops and resizes.

    Source: passports.govt.nz and DIA's iPhone and Samsung guides, read 17 November 2026.
